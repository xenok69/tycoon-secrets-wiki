# Easter Eggs

Tycoon games are often full of small, non-essential surprises left in by the developers. These don't usually affect gameplay but are fun to discover and share.

A common trick is naming a building or vehicle after a specific in-joke term, which sometimes triggers a hidden cosmetic change or a brief animation. Try renaming your headquarters or flagship vehicle to see if anything unusual happens.

Keep an eye out for background details in loading screens or menu art too — developers sometimes hide references to earlier games in the series, or small jokes that only show up after reaching a certain milestone.
