After purchasing all walls in the **White Palace**, once of the walls show a series of 12 two digit numbers. The player will also notice that each wall corresponds to a transparent number at the top, from 0 to 7. Each set of numbers represents lines from the center of the tycoon to the wall with the corresponding numbers. After decoding each number, inputting the result into a Semaphore Cipher will reveal the code.
