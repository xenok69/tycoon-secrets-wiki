# City

The *city* is the third area you can unlock with money in the game. It costs $9000 and 27 Secrets to unlock, and can be unlocked at the button near the slide in White Palace
In this place you can find **20 Secrets** *(as of 24.07.2026)*.
Ranging anywhere from 1 to 6 Stud difficulty.

## Difficulties

The only <Difficulty level="1">**1 Stud difficulty**</Difficulty> only requires some searching.

The 3 <Difficulty level="2">**2 Stud difficulties**</Difficulty> in this area requires somewhat basic understanding of ciphers, and roblox.

The 5 <Difficulty level="3">**3 Stud difficulties**</Difficulty> requires you to have a multitude of skills, such as decoding, problem solving, and obbying.

The 8 <Difficulty level="4">**4 Stud difficulties**</Difficulty> requires you to be able to have relatively advanced knowledge of ciphers, and may require you to get lucky sometimes :c .

When solving the 2 <Difficulty level="5">**5 Stud difficulties**</Difficulty> in this area, you **MUST** have good knowledge of ciphers.

Good luck when solving the only <Difficulty level="6">**6 Stud difficulty**</Difficulty> in this area.
