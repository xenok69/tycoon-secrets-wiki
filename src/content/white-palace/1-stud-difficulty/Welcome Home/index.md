## Hints

Use these hints to solve the secret. **Don't just enter the code** since that ruins the game for you and others.
Please try to actually solve the secret, as that's way more fun anyways.

* Starting point: ||The gate outside the tycoon.||
* Hint 1: ||Do you see any text on the walls?||
* Hint 2: ||Is there anything out of place on the text?||
* Hint 3: ||It is not the text with your name.||

## Solution

*Note: Only use the solution if you are truly stuck or on the edge of giving up.*

??>

To get to the solution, you need to look at the **'White Palace'** text above the gate.
If you look a bit closer, you can see that the first **a** in Palace is actually darker than the rest.
If you were to click on that darkened **a** you will see that the text transforms into a new word.
After the transformation, you'll get the final code which you can input.

**Code: whiteplace**
