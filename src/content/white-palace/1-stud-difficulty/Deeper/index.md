## Solution

*Note: Only use the solution if you are truely stuck or on the edge of giving up.*

If you were to go to the conveyor belt inside the tycoon, you would see a hole at the end of it.
**Don't go into the hole** but stay on top and try to look inside the hole.
If you look around closely with your camera in third person, you can see a text on the brim of the hole.

**Code: deep3rcod3s**
