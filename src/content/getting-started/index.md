# Getting Started

Welcome to the **Tycoon Secrets Wiki** — a community-maintained collection of tips, strategies, and hidden mechanics for tycoon-style management and business simulation games. Whether you're building your first factory or optimizing a sprawling empire, you'll find something useful here.

This wiki is organized into a handful of top-level topics covering production, logistics, and the less obvious tricks that experienced players rely on. New pages get added as more secrets are discovered, so check back often.

![Wiki overview diagram](assets/overview-diagram.svg)

## Where to start

If you're new, we recommend browsing the [Factories](#/factories) section first — it covers the fundamentals of setting up production chains before moving on to more advanced automation and logistics topics.

## How this wiki works

Each page here is short and focused on a single idea. Longer topics are split into subpages so you can jump straight to what you need. Look out for the **Hidden Secrets** section for things the games don't tell you directly.
